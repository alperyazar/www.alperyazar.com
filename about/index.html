Hi, I am Alper!

I’ve been passionate about electronics and computers since I was 10. Early in my career, I mostly designed hardware: circuit boards, FPGAs, SoCs, MCUs, and a variety of embedded platforms. These days, my work spans multiple layers of the stack: analyzing project and customer requirements, contributing to system architecture, deriving requirements for hardware and software components, and reviewing schematics and layouts. Depending on the project, I still do hands-on FPGA design (usually Xilinx/AMD) and embedded development across bare-metal, RTOS, and Linux. I’m particularly interested in applying DevOps and CI/CD practices to embedded systems, especially FPGA development workflows.

I earned my BSc and MSc degrees from the same department, METU EEE, in 2012 and 2015, respectively. My master’s thesis focused on statistical signal processing. As a PhD candidate, I worked on FPGA-accelerated cloud computing.

I sometimes consider myself a Full Stack Electronics Engineer.

Besides technology, I also love music and musical instruments. 🎵
